# Avathon

> Avathon (formerly SparkCognition, rebranded November 2024) is an industrial AI company.
> Its Industrial AI Platform layers a data platform, an industrial digital twin, AI modeling
> and application development to deliver predictive maintenance, asset performance management,
> visual intelligence and autonomy for physical operations across renewables, oil and gas,
> power and utilities, manufacturing, mining, aerospace and defense, maritime, rail, aviation,
> logistics, warehousing and retail.

## API surface

Avathon publishes no public developer portal, no API reference and no machine-readable
specification. Marketing material for the Avathon Industrial AI Platform describes an
application development SDK and APIs, but access to them is part of an enterprise engagement.
The only documentation surface, support.avathon.com/help, redirects (HTTP 307) to a HubSpot
member login. Agents evaluating Avathon for integration should expect to go through
https://avathon.com/contact-us-2/ or https://avathon.com/book-a-demo-2/ rather than a
self-service signup.
